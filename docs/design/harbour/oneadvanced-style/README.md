# Bordered panels and gridded tables

Owner's request (14 Nov 2026): give the Rota pages, and the platform generally, the bordered panel look of an
enterprise care system, using a screenshot of another vendor's product as a reference. We copy the structure
only. Code, branding, wording and colours are ClearCare's own, and nothing from the reference vendor
(logo, names, assets, data) is used or stored in this repository.

What changed (styling and panel titles only, no data, permission or behaviour changes):

- Panels have a clearer outline, a taller solid title bar and a round "open full screen" button.
- Tool rows use larger dark icons.
- Tables inside panels are ruled into a grid, with a line between every row and column, a grey header row and
  fixed row heights. Calendar boards and the week grid keep their own look.
- Ten Rota register pages and the service users list now sit in titled panels, colour-coded by meaning:
  risks and incidents red, staff and readiness purple, care plans teal, timesheets green, mileage orange,
  exceptions amber, visit notes blue, run routes navy.
- Daily Rota keeps its layout.

Screenshots in this folder use demo data only.
