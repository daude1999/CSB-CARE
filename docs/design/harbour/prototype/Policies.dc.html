2Purpose

This policy sets out how we keep the people we support safe from abuse and neglect, and what every member of staff must do when they are worried about someone.

It explains how to recognise harm, how to respond in the moment, and how to pass a concern on so that it reaches the right people quickly.

3Scope

Everyone who works for the service, including agency staff, volunteers and office staff. It applies in people's homes, in the office and when travelling between visits.

4Objectives

  1. Every concern is recorded the same day and passed to the safeguarding lead.
  2. Concerns that involve a risk of immediate harm are reported to the local authority within one hour.
  3. The person and, where appropriate, their family are kept informed and involved.

5Policy

We do not tolerate abuse in any form. Staff must not promise to keep a disclosure secret, and must never investigate a concern themselves.

5.4Local safeguarding contactsLocal

Liverpool Safeguarding Adults Board and Wirral Safeguarding Adults Partnership contact details are kept here. This section belongs to Care Solution Bureau and is never changed by a central update.

6Procedure

If you are worried about someone during a visit, make them safe first, then call the office. Record what you saw and heard in the person's own words.
