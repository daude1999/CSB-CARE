# Version 1 defect register

Defects found during the V1 operational readiness pass (brief of 30 Oct 2026), on branch `claude/project-thread-emflic`. Each was reproduced before it was fixed, and each fix is covered by an automated test unless the row says otherwise. Severity: **Critical** (wrong or unusable output, or failure at real scale), **Major** (a workflow is wrong or missing), **Minor** (a visible flaw with a workaround).

| ID | Area | Defect and root cause | Severity | Status | Fix and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D01 | Policy Library PDF | Text in the preview overlapped and was distorted (mixed case, squashed). Root cause: the PDF named the standard Helvetica font without embedding it, and the browser viewer (PDF.js, `useSystemFonts` on by default) drew it with whatever "Helvetica" the device had. Reproduced by installing a different font under that name. | Critical | Fixed | Liberation Sans (SIL OFL) embedded in every PDF; viewers set `useSystemFonts: false`. `tests/unit/document-rendering.test.ts` checks the font is embedded and no standard Helvetica remains. Commit 04f4f7e. |
| D02 | Policy PDF layout | Headings and table header rows could sit alone at the foot of a page. | Minor | Fixed | Keep-with-next for headings and table headers; tested for every page of three fixtures. |
| D03 | Policy PDF | Footer page numbers did not match the viewer's page counter (cover not counted). | Minor | Fixed | "Page n of N" counts the cover as page 1; tested. |
| D04 | Policy PDF and Word | Starter policies with no approval record printed an approver line that read as approved. | Major | Fixed | An explicit "Approval" row: "Approved", "Not yet approved (starter content)" or "Not yet approved"; tested in PDF and DOCX. |
| D05 | Policy viewer | The preview was a fixed 306 px tall, and pages drawn into one shared canvas could race and show the wrong page. | Major | Fixed | Responsive viewer (`clamp(440px, 100vh − 230px, 980px)`), full-screen reader, one canvas per page, lazy rendering. e2e `library.spec.ts`. |
| D06 | Policy viewer print | Print used a blob iframe, which the site's security headers block. | Major | Fixed | Pages are rendered to images and printed from the page itself; e2e checks one image per page. |
| D07 | Policy inspector | "More policy actions" opened downwards and was cut off by the taller viewer. | Minor | Fixed | Menu opens upwards there; e2e clicks "Archive policy". |
| D08 | Rostering | No two-way shift swap. | Major (gap) | Built | `src/server/rota/swap.ts`: both visits and carers row-locked in a fixed order, eligibility both ways, all or nothing, audit and visit events. `tests/integration/swap.test.ts` (5 tests incl. overlap, training and two coordinators at once). |
| D09 | Advanced Planner | A visit could not be dragged to another day. | Major (gap) | Built | `moveVisit` (same time, optional new carer, one transaction). `tests/integration/planner-moves.test.ts`. |
| D10 | Run routes | A stop could not be moved straight from one route to another. | Minor (gap) | Built | `moveToRoute` (same week only, audited). Tested in `planner-moves.test.ts`. |
| D11 | Finance CSV | Negative amounts (credits) were exported as text, so spreadsheet totals ignored them. Root cause: the formula guard treated a leading minus as a formula. | Major | Fixed | One shared writer `src/lib/csv.ts`: plain numbers stay numbers; text starting `= + - @`, tab or CR is neutralised. `tests/unit/csv.test.ts`. |

| D13 | Demo data | In the demo, every carer was "Requires review", so the allocation picker never showed an "Eligible" carer. Root cause (measured): the starter readiness matrix includes "Safeguarding policy read and acknowledged", and demo carers have no sign-in, so they cannot acknowledge. | D15 | Rota publish | Publishing a full-scale week (1,204 visits) failed with "Transaction already closed" after 5 s. Root cause: publish re-checked every future booking of every carer on the week (all later weeks too), inside a 5 s transaction, and silently stopped at 2,000 bookings. | Critical (at scale) | Fixed | Publish re-checks the week's own visits, with no cap, and has a 120 s limit like the build. | D16 | Unassigned Visits | The page took 3.3–3.9 s at full scale (two measurements). Same results as the single-visit ranking (tested in `uat-scenarios.test.ts`). |

## Not defects, recorded so they are not reported again

- **Carers without a sign-in are "Requires review"** while the safeguarding policy requirement is active: they cannot acknowledge a policy. Give them a sign-in (linked by hand) or exempt them.
- **Timesheet submit refuses lines with exceptions** until someone has checked them. This is the intended control, measured in the bulk submit (6 submitted, 46 refused with the reason).
- **Building a week leaves a slot empty** when the template's carer has a hard stop (DBS review due, training expired, medication competency missing). It never books an ineligible carer; the slot shows on Unassigned Visits.
